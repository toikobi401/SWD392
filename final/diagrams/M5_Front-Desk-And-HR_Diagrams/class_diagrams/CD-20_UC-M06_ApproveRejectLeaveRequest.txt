    REJECTED

LeaveRequest --> LeaveType

note bottom of LeaveController
    Self-message operations fold middleware, leave service, policy and audit
    logic into this boundary, as in SD-20; they are not extra endpoints.
    Supporting Employee/Shift work remains within these operations.
end note
note bottom of ApprovalsPage
    StaffPages.tsx: LeaveDecision is part of ApprovalsPage.
    Page actions name UI interactions; onError/onSuccess are real callbacks.
    Query chain operations are shown on their owning model.
end note
note bottom of EmailService
    Represents NotificationProxy.sendDecisionNotice's EMAIL destination.
    With no NOTIFICATION_API_URL, development mode logs the message locally.
end note
